This is a **draft**, visible in local development and Pages CMS, excluded from the public build.

Replace the title, description and text. Add a cover image and its description, or create a gallery. When the entry is ready, turn off **Draft** and save it on the production branch.

## Content formats

- **Note:** a short thought or update.
- **Article:** a longer text with headings and images.
- **Gallery:** an entry with an image collection and captions.

The filename becomes the permanent URL unless you provide a custom slug. Changing a title does not require changing that URL.
